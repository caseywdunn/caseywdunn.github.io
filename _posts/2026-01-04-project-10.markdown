description: I am Curator of <a href="https://peabody.yale.edu/explore/collections/invertebrate-zoology">Invertebrate Zoology</a> at the Yale Peabody Museum. The collection approaches 300,000 specimen lots, representing about 3 million individuals, thousands of which are the type specimens of species new to science. Its strengths include western Atlantic invertebrates, tropical reef animals, and a worldwide sponge collection, with material dating back to the late 1800s.
